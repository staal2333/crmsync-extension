Getting Started with CRMSYNC

Welcome! This guide will help you set up CRMSYNC and start syncing contacts from Gmail to your CRM in just a few minutes.

Installation

1 Install the Chrome Extension

Visit the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome". The extension will install in about 30 seconds.

2 Pin the Extension

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then click the pin icon next to CRMSYNC. This keeps the extension easily accessible.

3 Open Gmail

Navigate to Gmail. CRMSYNC will automatically activate and start detecting contacts.

Create Your Account

While you can use CRMSYNC without an account (guest mode), creating an account unlocks:

  • Cloud backup of your contacts
  • Sync across multiple devices
  • CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce)
  • Higher contact limits

Tip: Sign up with Google for the fastest setup - just one click!

Capturing Your First Contact

  1. Open any email in Gmail
  2. CRMSYNC will detect the sender's contact information
  3. A notification appears: "New Contact Found"
  4. Click Approve to save, or Reject to skip
  5. Your contact is now saved!

Pro Tip: Enable "Auto-Approve" in settings to skip the manual approval step for trusted contacts.

HubSpot Integration

Connect HubSpot to sync contacts directly to your CRM:

  1. Click the CRMSYNC extension icon
  2. Go to the Integrations tab
  3. Click Connect HubSpot
  4. Authorize access in the popup window
  5. Done! You can now push contacts to HubSpot

Salesforce Integration

Connect Salesforce to sync contacts:

  1. Click the CRMSYNC extension icon
  2. Go to the Integrations tab
  3. Click Connect Salesforce
  4. Log in to your Salesforce account
  5. Authorize the connection

Auto-Detection Settings

CRMSYNC automatically extracts:

  • Names - From email headers and signatures
  • Email addresses - From sender info
  • Phone numbers - From signatures
  • Company names - From signatures and email domains
  • Job titles - From signatures

Exclusions

Block specific contacts from being tracked:

  • Exclude Domains: Block all emails from a domain (e.g., your own company)
  • Exclude Names: Block specific people
  • Exclude Phones: Block specific phone numbers

Important: Add your own email domain to exclusions to avoid tracking your coworkers!

Exporting Contacts

Export your contacts as CSV:

  1. Open the extension popup
  2. Click the Export button
  3. Choose which contacts to export (all, today, etc.)
  4. A CSV file will download automatically

Troubleshooting: Contacts Not Detected

If CRMSYNC isn't detecting contacts:

  • Make sure you're on Gmail (mail.google.com)
  • Refresh the Gmail page
  • Check that the extension is enabled in Chrome
  • Ensure the contact isn't in your exclusion list
  • Try clicking on the email to open it fully

Troubleshooting: Sync Errors

If you see sync errors:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Re-authenticate your CRM connection
  • Wait a few minutes (you may have hit rate limits)
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my email content stored?

No. We only extract contact information. Your email content is never stored or transmitted.

Can I use CRMSYNC on multiple computers?

Yes! Sign in with your account and your contacts will sync across devices.

What happens if I uninstall?

If you have an account, your contacts are safely stored in the cloud. Just reinstall and sign in to get them back.
