Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes! All paid plans come with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start. Cancel anytime during the trial.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

You'll see a prompt to upgrade. Your existing contacts are safe - you just can't add new ones until next month or you upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel with one click from your account settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer full refunds within 14 days of purchase if you're not satisfied. Just contact support.

Is my data secure?

We use enterprise-grade encryption, secure OAuth for CRM connections, and never store your email content. See our privacy policy for details.

What CRMs do you support?

Currently HubSpot and Salesforce. We're adding more CRMs based on user demand. Request yours via support!
